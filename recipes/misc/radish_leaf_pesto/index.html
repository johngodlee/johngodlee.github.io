Radish leaf pesto

Ingredients

  • Large handful of radish tops
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 0.5 large lemon, juiced
  • 0.5 cup sliced almonds, chopped macadamia nuts, pistachios or pine nuts
  • 0.5 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 0.3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  • Combine all the ingredients in a food processor, or in a large bowl with a stick blender.
  • Process until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.
  • Add additional olive oil to achieve a thick sauce consistency.
  • Season to taste with salt and black pepper.
